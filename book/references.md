# Sources and further reading

This tutorial adapts ideas and examples from:

- [The BlackJAX Sampling Book](https://blackjax-devs.github.io/sampling-book/)
  and its [source repository](https://github.com/blackjax-devs/sampling-book):
  the MyST book structure, executable lessons, Pathfinder introduction, and
  GitHub Pages publishing pattern.
- [The Nested Sampling Book](https://handley-lab.co.uk/nested-sampling-book/)
  and its [source repository](https://github.com/handley-lab/nested-sampling-book):
  the line-fitting example, named PyTrees, the Distrax prior pattern, and the
  route toward nested sampling.
- [BlackJAX documentation](https://blackjax-devs.github.io/blackjax/) and the
  [BlackJAX repository](https://github.com/blackjax-devs/blackjax).
- [Distrax](https://github.com/google-deepmind/distrax), used to keep prior
  sampling and log-density evaluation together.
- [Pathfinder: Parallel Quasi-Newton Variational
  Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.03782).
- [Nested Slice Sampling: Vectorized Nested Sampling for GPU-Accelerated
  Inference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.23252).
- [Nested Sampling with Slice-within-Gibbs: Efficient Evidence Calculation for
  Hierarchical Bayesian Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17414) and the
  [SwiG implementation](https://github.com/yallup/swig).
